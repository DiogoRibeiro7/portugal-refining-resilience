# Proposed report outline

**From Refining Capacity to Import Dependence: Portugal's Diesel and Gasoline Market, 2005–2024**

The report should tell one empirical story: how a refinery system that gained diesel conversion capability in 2013 and then lost one refinery in 2021 changed its physical product balance and resilience, with 2022 treated as a stress event rather than as clean post-treatment evidence.
